[Marlowe, the cellar spider](/2012/09/domestic-science/ "Domestic science") who has been living behind my beside cabinet for the last 18 months, disappeared a few weeks ago, but I wasn't too worried as they'd done that before and then turned up again a few weeks later, fatter and of greater span.

On Monday, however, Alex found Marlowe's mortal remains beneath the bathroom radiator. Marlowe is no more.

Rather than an undignified flushing or binning, I thought an undignified burning was in order, given the Viking weather the UK is currently experiencing:

[<img src="/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/marlowe_longboat-300x225.jpg" class="aligncenter size-medium wp-image-605" width="300" height="225" alt="Marlowe&#39;s longboat (CC-BY-SA-3.0 Steve Cook)" />](/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/marlowe_longboat.jpg)Marlowe was well-supplied with grave goods - origami flowers, a votive offering of a dead fly, and a ceremonial hemp web - for their journey we know not whither:

[<img src="/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/marlowe_grave_goods-300x225.jpg" class="aligncenter size-medium wp-image-608" width="300" height="225" alt="Marlowe&#39;s grave goods (CC-BY-SA-3.0 Steve Cook)" />](/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/marlowe_grave_goods.jpg)

Purifying fire released their elements back to the air from which they came (somewhat indirectly, being a secondary consumer):

[<img src="/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/marlowe_funeral_pyre-300x225.jpg" class="aligncenter size-medium wp-image-607" width="300" height="225" alt="Marlowe&#39;s funeral pyre (CC-BY-SA-3.0 Steve Cook)" />](/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/marlowe_funeral_pyre.jpg)Ashes to ashes, dust to dust.

[<img src="/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/marlowe_ashes_to_ashes-300x225.jpg" class="aligncenter size-medium wp-image-606" width="300" height="225" alt="Marlowe ashes to ashes (CC-BY-SA-3.0 Steve Cook)" />](/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/marlowe_ashes_to_ashes.jpg)

Farewell Marlowe. May your spiderlings inherit the Earth.
