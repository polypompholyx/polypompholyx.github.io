The minute I found out there was a botanic garden in the north of Gran Canaria as well, a return visit became inevitable.

The [Jardín Botánico Canario "Viera y Clavijo"](http://www.jardincanario.org/) is in the outskirts of Las Palmas, and is much larger than its [Maspaloman sibling](/2017/04/more-botanical-baggings/). As well as showcasing a Canary Island pine woodland, it also has a impressive path carved into the side of the cliff that overlooks the site. This affords splendid views across the gardens - assuming you can stomach them.

Entry is free!

Botanerd highlights: the cactus and succulent garden is fantastic. It is large, and has many mature specimens of cactuses, *Euphorbia*, [*Pachypodium*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pachypodium), and even a Madagascan [*Alluaudia*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alluaudia).  Some of the specimens are vast: this [*Pachycerus weberi*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pachycereus) is about 8 m high, and many of the *Euphorbia* are similarly imposing.
