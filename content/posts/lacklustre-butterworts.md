I'd like to be more excited about this than I am, but *Pinguicula agnata* is not the most crowd-pleasing of my butterworts, even whilst in flower (as it currently is). Ho hum. [Strange specific epithet though](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrilineality "Agnatic kinship at Wikipedia"). No idea why [Casper](http://www.omnisterra.com/bot/cp_home.cgi?name=pinguicula+agnata&submit=Submit&search=accepted "Pinguicula agnata at the Carnivorous Plant Database") (whoever s/he might have been) thought it appropriate. [Could be worse though](http://www.curioustaxonomy.net/ "Curious taxonomy"), I guess.
