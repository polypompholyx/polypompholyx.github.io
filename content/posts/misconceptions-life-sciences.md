Last month, I was involved in teaching on a life science communication course, some of the results of which you can [see on Wikipedia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:Polypompholyx "Polypompholyx's userpage on Wikipedia"). One of our guest speakers was [Alom Shaha](http://alomshaha.com/ "Alom Shaha"), who demonstrated the misconceptions students have about *e.g.* the forces acting on a ball in flight:

This got me thinking that it might be worthwhile cataloguing some of the commoner misconceptions my students have in the biochemistry and biology topics I teach. Like the [Perl tutorial](/perl/ "Perl"), this will be a work in progress: I'll post larger articles on these topics as I find time.  

- [Succinate dehydrogenase (SDH) produces reduced flavin (FADH<sub>2</sub>) as a product](/2013/04/misconceptions-sdh/ "The product of succinate dehydrogenase is ubiquinol not reduced flavin")
- [The end product of glycolysis is pyruvate](/2013/04/misconceptions-end-products/ "Metabolic pathways don’t really have end products")
- The sign of standard free energy change (∆G<sup>0</sup>) determines whether a reaction will proceed
- [An enzyme lowers the activation energy for a reaction](/2013/08/misconceptions-enzyme-energy/ "Enzymes provide alternative routes to product with a lower activation energy")
- [An enzyme increases the rate of product formation](/2013/04/misconceptions-enzyme-rate/ "Enzymes don't necessarily increase the rate at which products are formed")
- [Enzymes in cells can be modelled using the Michaelis Menten equation](/2013/05/misconceptions-mmm/ "The Michaelis-Menten model is not applicable to most enzymes in a cell")
- An electrogenic pump is required to generate a membrane potential
- The hydrophobic effect is a result of van der Waals attraction between fatty molecules
- 'Gene' has a rigorous definition
- mRNA is a bland photocopy of the information in DNA
- [A *P* value is the probability that the null hypothesis is true](/2015/08/r-statistical-power/)
- The answer to any question about improving an experiment is 'more repeats'
- Bacteria take up environmental DNA by transformation in order to gain new features
- The problem with Lamarckism is the inheritance of acquired characteristics
- The term 'missing link' is meaningful
- Evolution is synonymous with natural selection
- [Organisms evolve adaptations to increase their fitness](/2016/05/misconceptions_evolution_teleology/)
- [Some organisms, like cycads, are living fossils](/2012/04/why-living-fossils-need-to-die/ "Why living fossils need to die")
- [Some groups of organisms, like magnolias, branched off early in the history of life](/2012/07/magnolia-misunderstanding/ "The magnolia misunderstanding")
- [Some organisms, like *Psilotum*, are primitive](/2013/04/organism-of-the-week-11/ "Organism of the week #11 – Not even primitive")
- [Junk DNA is a disproven myth](/2012/09/a-brief-history-of-rubbish/ "A brief history of rubbish")
