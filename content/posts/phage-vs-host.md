For a recent schools' outreach day, I put together [a card-game based around the arms-races](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/phage_game.pptx) that develop between bacterial hosts and their viruses (bacteriophages). It's mostly just a bit of fun, but if anyone finds it useful or can suggest improvements (or just make them! I release this under a CC-BY-SA-4.0 license) I'd be happy to hear them.
