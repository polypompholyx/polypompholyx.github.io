Previous winners: *[Vorticella](/2012/10/glockentierchen/ "Glockentierchen")* and *[Lacrymaria](/2013/10/teardrops-of-the-swan/ "Teardrops of the swan")*. The Ciliata really are the phylum that keeps on giving.
