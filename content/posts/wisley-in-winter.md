It's not *really* a botanic garden, but the [Royal Horticultural Society's gardens at Wisley](https://www.rhs.org.uk/gardens/wisley) is near enough as makes no difference. We visited in what should have been the dead of winter, but which in reality was this weird sprautumn mash-up that is now December in the UK. The heather garden was particularly pretty, despite the wind:

<figure class="wp-caption aligncenter" style="max-width:225px"><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Wisley_heather_garden.jpg" rel="attachment wp-att-2000"><img loading="lazy" src="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Wisley_heather_garden-225x300.jpg" alt="RHS Wisley heather garden [CC-BY-SA-3.0 Steve Cook]" /></a><figcaption>RHS Wisley heather garden</figcaption></figure>

At £12, the entry price is a bit steep: even Kew does reduced rates when most of the plants are underground, sleeping off the summer's excesses.

Botanerd highlights included the biggest pine-cone I have yet added to my collection (yes, it *is* a collection, *not* a sickness)...

<figure class="wp-caption aligncenter" style="max-width:207px"><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Pinus_×_holfordiana.jpg" rel="attachment wp-att-2001"><img loading="lazy" src="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Pinus_×_holfordiana-207x300.jpg" alt="Pinus × holfordiana cone [CC-BY-SA-3.0 Steve Cook]" /></a><figcaption>Holford pine (<em>Pinus × holfordiana</em>) cone - nothing satisfies like a 12 inch <em>Pinus</em></figcaption></figure>

...and the - apparently legal - purchase of class A drugs from the attached garden centre:

<figure class="wp-caption aligncenter" style="max-width:300px"><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Lophophora_williamsii_RHS.jpg" rel="attachment wp-att-2002"><img loading="lazy" src="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Lophophora_williamsii_RHS-300x300.jpg" alt="Lophophora williamsii [CC-BY-SA-3.0 Steve Cook]" /></a><figcaption>Peyote cactus (<em>Lophophora williamsii</em>)</figcaption></figure>

From what I gather, *owning* a peyote cactus is legal in the UK unless you 'prepare it for consumption', which magically transforms it into a 7 year jail term. This presumably includes accidentally letting it die and dry out into a button whilst on holiday. I may be having a teesy bit of buyers' regret.
